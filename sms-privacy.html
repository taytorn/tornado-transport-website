Recruiting / SMS Privacy Policy

Prepared November 24, 2026 · Version TT-SMS-2026-11-24-v1

The SMS program is being prepared. Text messages will begin only after carrier approval and operational opt-out controls are ready.

Scope. This policy applies only to information Tornado Transport LLC receives through the recruiting/SMS email signup flow and Recruiting SMS Program. It does not replace unrelated Tornado website policies or the separate notices and terms of Bee Exposed Marketing, Tenstreet/IntelliApp, or a hiring carrier’s application process.

Page and email information. The signup page prepares a consent-email preview. It does not submit or store the form entries. If you send the email, Tornado receives the information you send, which may include your name, email address, mobile phone number, YES/NO selections, and disclosed terms. We use it to process your choices, provide the recruiting texts you authorize, and handle help and unsubscribe requests. Email transmission is handled by the mailbox services you and Tornado use. A draft that you do not send does not enroll you.

Consent records. We retain the actual received email, including the exact disclosures, version, policy/page URLs, mobile number, and each YES/NO selection. We record its receipt time and message identifier and maintain later consent changes and unsubscribe records. A preview-generation time is not proof of consent; the received email is the evidence used to process enrollment. This policy does not specify a fixed retention period.

Mobile information and SMS consent. No mobile information collected through this signup flow or program will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or transfer SMS subscriber lists or authorize another business to market to you using this program’s mobile information.

We may use service providers for email, support, and text-message delivery. Mobile information may be provided to operational service providers only as needed to perform those services, not for their own marketing. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the Text Message services. Mailbox providers process the email you send as part of transmitting and storing that email. Tornado will not forward the consent email, SMS choices, disclosure records, or consent evidence to Bee Exposed Marketing or hiring carriers as an application or referral.

Separate external applications. The link https://intelliapp.driverapponline.com/c/truckers4hire?r=UthemesT opens a separate Bee Exposed Marketing application. It may send your application to a relevant hiring carrier. The application may independently collect your phone number and other personal information under its own notices. A hiring carrier may therefore receive a mobile number you provide in that application; this policy does not promise otherwise. That independent application submission is not a transfer of Tornado’s SMS consent. Tornado’s consent does not authorize Bee Exposed Marketing or a hiring carrier to send its own marketing texts. Not all roles are employment with Tornado.

Choices and contact. You can apply or request a call without enrolling in SMS. To unsubscribe from Tornado Recruiting SMS, reply STOP or contact us. For help or privacy questions under this policy, email taylor@tornado-transport.com or call +1 470 614 9045. See the SMS Program Terms.
