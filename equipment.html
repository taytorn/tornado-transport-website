01 / Truck types

A model name is
just the starting point.

Truck year, configuration and features can differ within the same model. Confirm the actual unit and assignment details for the position you’re considering.

Truck model references

  • Freightliner Cascadia
  • Volvo VNL
  • Mack Anthem
  • Peterbilt 579
  • Kenworth T680
  • International ProStar

These names are reference examples from this guide, not a current fleet inventory or a promise that a particular truck is available.

Confirm the setup, not just the badge.

  • Truck assignmentAsk which model, year and configuration apply to the role, and how equipment is assigned.
  • Cab & sleeperAsk whether the role uses a day cab or sleeper, and which storage and in-cab amenities are included.
  • PowertrainConfirm the engine, transmission and any role-specific operating requirements.
  • Driver technologyAsk about safety systems, navigation, communication tools and electronic logging equipment.

02 / Trailer types

Different freight.
Different expectations.

These are general trailer categories, not an equipment availability list. Review the freight and handling requirements attached to each role.

Enclosed freight

Dry Van Trailers

Enclosed trailers commonly used for packaged goods, palletized freight and general merchandise.

Questions to ask

  • What are the loading and unloading responsibilities?
  • What trailer dimensions, door configuration and cargo-securement requirements apply?

Temperature-sensitive freight

Refrigerated Trailers

Temperature-controlled trailers commonly used for fresh produce, frozen foods and other temperature-sensitive freight.

Questions to ask

  • What temperature-setting and monitoring responsibilities apply?
  • What are the procedures for the refrigeration unit, trailer cleaning and delivery appointments?

Open-deck freight

Flatbed Trailers

Open-deck trailers commonly used for building materials, machinery, steel and lumber.

Questions to ask

  • What securement, tarping and physical tasks are required?
  • What trailer configuration, loading methods and training requirements apply?

Dimensions, load limits, features and freight requirements must be confirmed for the assigned trailer and load. General category descriptions are not specifications for equipment offered with a position.

03 / Equipment support

Ask before
you get behind the wheel.

Maintenance, breakdown procedures and equipment preferences are role-specific. A clear conversation helps you understand the responsibilities and support attached to the position.

Email Your Equipment Questions ↗
How is maintenance coordinated?

Ask who schedules preventive maintenance, how to report an equipment concern and what to do when a unit needs service. Confirm inspection and reporting procedures with the employer for your specific role.

What happens if equipment breaks down?

Confirm breakdown reporting procedures, roadside support contacts and downtime policies. Do not assume a particular support schedule, service network or pay arrangement.

Which in-cab features are included?

Confirm safety technology, navigation, communication tools and sleeper amenities for the assigned unit. A model name does not guarantee a particular feature or configuration.

Can I request a particular truck?

Ask how assignments are made and whether equipment preferences can be considered. The model and setup may depend on run type, location and operational needs; no particular truck is promised by this guide.
