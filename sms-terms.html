SMS Program Terms

Prepared November 24, 2026 · Version TT-SMS-2026-11-24-v2

The SMS program is being prepared. Text messages will begin only after mobile-network SMS approval and operational opt-out controls are ready.

These terms apply only to Tornado Transport LLC’s Recruiting SMS Program, not a separate hiring carrier’s messaging program or the external application service.

Messages and enrollment. If you affirmatively authorize informational recruiting texts in the consent email you send us, we will text you about your recruiting inquiry, requested application assistance, and interview or call scheduling. If you separately authorize job-opportunity texts, we will send recurring CDL-A job-opportunity and promotional recruiting messages about Tornado and partner-carrier roles. Not all roles are employment with Tornado. Messages may use automated technology. We will send only the categories you authorized. The signup page prepares a reviewable email; a generated or unsent draft is not consent.

Your choice. Enrollment is optional and is not a condition of applying, being considered for a job, requesting a call, or purchasing anything. Providing a phone number, requesting a call, or applying does not by itself grant SMS consent. Your consent authorizes Tornado’s messages only, not another business’s texts.

Frequency and charges. Message frequency varies by selected category and recruiting activity. Job-opportunity messages are recurring. Message and data rates may apply to messages sent to you and messages you send to us. Ask your wireless provider about your plan and charges.

Opt out. Reply STOP to a program message to unsubscribe from all Tornado Recruiting SMS categories. We will stop program messages across the numbers used for this program. You may receive one non-promotional unsubscribe confirmation. You can also ask us to stop texts at taylor@tornado-transport.com or by calling +1 470 260 8355. Opting out of SMS does not withdraw your job application. To rejoin, send a fresh consent email with affirmative category selections through the signup flow. An application, callback request, or unsent email draft does not restart texts.

Help. Reply HELP for assistance, email taylor@tornado-transport.com, or call +1 470 260 8355. Our website is https://tornado-transport.com.

Delivery. Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages. Here, “carriers” means wireless service providers, not hiring carriers.

Privacy. See the Recruiting and SMS Privacy Policy.
